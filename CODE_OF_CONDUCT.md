# Code of conduct

Swarm follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies in this repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere someone represents the project.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, and criticise the work and not the person. Harassment, personal attacks and publishing someone's private details are not accepted.

## Reporting a problem

Contact the maintainer privately, `@jntegz` on GitHub. Reports are kept confidential, and the maintainer will not act on a report about themselves alone.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, or warn or ban someone from the project, depending on how serious the problem is.

Security vulnerabilities go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
