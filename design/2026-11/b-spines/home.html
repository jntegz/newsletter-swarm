Step 2: DMARC set to quarantine

Your record says p=none. Add this TXT record at your DNS host, then check again.

Type
TXT
Host

Value
v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; rua=mailto:dmarc+fn@reports.swarm.example
DNS changes can take up to an hour to appear.
