Swarm redesign: three directions

Each direction has its own look and its own layout, with a Home dashboard and the post editor. They share one renamed, grouped menu and the same editor fixes, so you are comparing the presentation, not the features. All numbers are sample data.

A · Line

The menu is drawn as a transit line, and you are the yellow stop. Home opens with what needs you and what's next; the last send follows as a route: queued, delivered, opened, clicked.

  • LayoutDark side rail. The editor's send panel is a three-stop route on the right.
  • ColourYellow only means "you are here".
  • TypeAtkinson Hyperlegible Next, a font designed for legibility

Trade-off: the most familiar layout of the three. It stands out through the line, not the layout.

What all three share

These changes apply whichever direction you pick.

Finding your way

  • The menu uses plain words, in five groups: Write, Audience, Grow, Earn and Publish.
  • Publications move to a switcher at the top instead of a menu item.
  • Sending & delivery is moved out from under Channel pages.
  • Every page has a title and a breadcrumb, and Ctrl K jumps anywhere.
  • Home puts what needs you (setup steps or a problem) in the first view, then what's next and how the last send did. Growth and revenue come after.
  • The first run is a short path of four steps to the first send.

The post editor

  • "New post" asks where to start: blank, a template, or a duplicate of the last post.
  • Subject and preview text have visible labels and a live inbox preview.
  • Blocks are added from an Insert button in the toolbar or the + beside the current line, with no dialog per block.
  • Sending always goes Write, then Audience, then Check & send. The send button comes last and says why it's disabled.
  • There is one Preview (email desktop, email mobile, web) with a "View as" free or paid subscriber.
  • Split tests, website visibility and revisions are collapsed under More options.
