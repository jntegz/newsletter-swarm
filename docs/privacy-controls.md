# Tracking and retention

What Swarm records about each subscriber when they read, who can switch it off, and how long it keeps it. Code: `packages/engine/src/tracking-choices.ts` and `packages/engine/src/retention.ts`; tables: `migrations/0063_tracking_choices.sql`.

## What is recorded

When open tracking is on, each email carries a tiny image unique to that email. When click tracking is on, each link goes through your link domain first. Either way Swarm records, against the person: the time, the first part of their network address (the rest is cut off), their network (ASN) and their browser family. No cookies are set. Machine opens (privacy proxies such as Apple Mail) and link scanners are recorded and labelled, never counted as people.

From that, a publication with reader profiles on works out an [engagement score](engagement-scores.md) for each subscriber and the hour they usually read (for best-time sending).

## The publication's switches

**Sending & delivery > Tracking** has three switches. All three are **off** for a new publication: nothing is recorded against a person until the publisher turns a switch on. Get your subscribers' consent first (in the EU, a tracking image or tracked links in email generally need it), for example with a consent box on the sign-up form, and say in your privacy notice what you record. Publications made before this default changed keep the switches they had. Only admins and owners can change them, and every change is in the audit log.

| Switch          | When it is off                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Open tracking   | No image in emails. Send reports show "Not tracked" instead of an open rate. Reader profiles turn off too, because they read opens.                                                                                                                                                    |
| Click tracking  | Links go straight to their address and nothing passes through Swarm. Send reports show no clicks, the click map is empty, and automations can't start on or wait for a click. Swarm does not count clicks without the person, because a click only means something tied to a delivery. |
| Reader profiles | No engagement scores and no best-hour profiles: existing ones are deleted at the next daily pass. Segment conditions on the score match nobody, best-time sending falls back to the chosen time, and the inactive-reader cleanup does not run.                                         |

A switch turned off applies at once, also to emails that already went out (a pixel or link in an old email is no longer recorded) and to the rest of a send in progress. Turning one back on applies to sends started afterwards.

The inactive-reader cleanup (**Inactive readers** on the same page) only runs with open tracking and reader profiles on. It also counts only emails that had a pixel, so a period with open tracking off doesn't make anyone look inactive, never acts on a reader whose opens are hidden by their mail app, emails each reader 14 days before removing them, and does nothing when the period is longer than the engagement history kept (below). See [Audience](audience.md#cleaning-up-inactive-readers).

## The subscriber's own choice

**Subscriber settings** (the page linked from every email) has **Don't track my opens and clicks**. When someone ticks it:

- their emails have no image and plain links, and the unsubscribe link still works;
- they get no engagement score and no best sending hour, are never unsubscribed as inactive, and are never picked for "send again to people who didn't open";
- the opens and clicks recorded for them so far are deleted, with their open and click events. Their deliveries stay, without opened or clicked times, so their unsubscribe links keep working.

Send reports of past sends keep their numbers: what that person had added is kept as a count on the send and its links, without them (`carried_*` columns). On new sends, their emails count as sent and delivered but are left out of the open rate, and the report says how many there were.

Segment conditions such as "opened a post in the last 30 days" never match someone who asked not to be tracked. A segment of people who did **not** open includes them, so check it before using it to remove people.

They can untick it again; tracking starts from nothing.

Ticking it deletes history, so it needs a link emailed on request in the last hour: from the footer link of an email (which may have been forwarded) the page emails the subscriber a fresh link instead. Either change is mailed to their address.

## How long history is kept

**Settings > Privacy > Engagement history** sets, per workspace, how long deliveries, opens, clicks and per-send copies of addresses stay tied to a person: 6 months, 13 months (the default), 2 years or 3 years. Only admins and owners can change it.

There is no "keep forever". Engagement scores, best-time sending, segments and the check-up look back 90 days at most, and send reports keep their numbers once the person is removed. What does read person-linked history older than 90 days, so a shorter setting shows less of it:

| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Inactive-reader cleanup                                     | `membercenter.ts` `sunsetInactiveMembers`                          | The person's deliveries and their human opens and clicks over the whole period chosen (up to 2 years). It reads nothing sent before this setting's limit, even while the retention job hasn't reached it yet, and it does nothing (the setting says it is waiting) when this setting is shorter than the period. |
| Subscriber activity (the subscriber's page)                 | `routes/members.ts` activity                                       | Deliveries and clicks, to list the latest of them.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |

| Segment conditions "opened" or "clicked in the last N days" | `circles.ts` `compileRule`                                         | Deliveries in those N days (up to 10 years can be chosen; past this setting's limit they match nobody, or everybody for "not opened"). Used wherever a segment is: sending, previews, member lists and exports, automation triggers, branches and Smart Blocks.                                                  |

Activity events (`member_events`, read by webhooks and export destinations) are deleted 90 days after they are processed, so they don't outlast that.

Opens and clicks are tied to the person through their delivery, so one setting covers both. Three years covers an operator who has to show, for a dispute, that someone was sent a notice.

Past the limit, the jobs worker (every five minutes, in bounded batches, so a backlog on an old install is spread over hours):

| Record                                | What happens                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Deliveries                            | The subscriber reference, the address, the provider's message id and any error text are removed. Status and times stay. An unsubscribe-only reference is kept for 24 months (see below). |
| Opens and clicks                      | Network prefix, network and browser family are removed. The row stays, so link counts stay.                                                                                              |
| A finished post's per-send copies     | Deleted.                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| An automation email's per-send copies | The address and names are removed. The row stays, because it stops the automation sending that email to the same person twice.                                                           |
| Soft-bounce records                   | Deleted after 30 days whatever the setting, since only the last 7 days count toward blocking an address.                                                                                 |
| Suppressions (blocked addresses)      | Never touched. They are how Swarm keeps not mailing someone.                                                                                                                             |

Every number on a send report stays the same. After the limit, the subscriber's activity no longer lists those emails, a split test shows the result it was decided on rather than a per-version breakdown, and unsubscribe links in those emails keep working: just before a delivery to someone who can still be mailed loses its subscriber reference, Swarm keeps a separate unsubscribe-only reference (the delivery and the subscriber, nothing about opens or clicks; table `unsubscribe_refs`). It is read only by the unsubscribe page, goes when the subscriber is deleted or erased, and is deleted 24 months after it was written (`UNSUBSCRIBE_REF_MONTHS` in `retention.ts`). After that, the link's page says the email is too old, changes nothing (a one-click unsubscribe from a mail app gets status 410, not a false success) and points to a newer email or the subscriber's settings. A month here is 30.44 days.

## How long other records are kept

Code: `packages/engine/src/lifetimes.ts`; tables: `migrations/0108_record_lifetimes.sql`. The periods are constants or settings for the install owner to review.

| ----------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Someone who unsubscribed, bounced or complained | After **Settings > Privacy > Keep the full profile of people who left for** (1, 2 (default), 3 or 5 years, counted from when they left), their names, profile fields, tags, sign-up source, Champion code, unsubscribe reason and activity events are removed. The address, status, dates and consent records stay, so they are still never emailed and a return still needs their confirmation. API: `leftMemberMonths` on `/api/v1/workspaces/{workspaceId}/retention`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| A deleted publication                           | Restorable for 30 days. Its export destinations stop running at once. 60 days after the delete (`CHANNEL_PURGE_DAYS`), its subscribers are deleted (delivery counts stay) and the addresses in its checkouts, orders, Gift Passes, trial claims and sponsor bookings are removed; amounts stay for your books. Then its stored files go too: everything under the publication in the media bucket (images, goods' files), the imports bucket and the exports bucket, up to 1,000 objects per bucket every five minutes (`purgeDeletedChannelFiles`). From the delete on, its automations take no further step and start no new runs, and its events reach no webhook (except `member.deleted`); events from that time are not sent after a restore. Until the purge an admin can still file data requests for its readers in the Privacy Center.                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Payment references after a deletion request     | A payment's Stripe or PayPal id and a checkout's Stripe session id stay on the rows kept for your books, without the person, for **Settings > Privacy > Keep payment references after a deletion request** (6, 7, 8 or 10 (default) years after each payment, `paymentRecordYears`). Then they are removed and the amounts and dates stay. Choose the period your country's tax law sets for accounting records. See [GDPR](gdpr.md#what-swarm-does-not-do-for-you).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Teammates' sign-ins                             | Ended sessions (with their browser and network prefix), used or expired sign-in codes (with the address typed), and invitations never accepted are deleted 30 days after they ended (`OPERATOR_RECORD_DAYS`). A teammate's own account activity (sign-ins and factor changes, with the browser and network prefix, shown only to them) is deleted after 400 days (`ACCOUNT_EVENT_DAYS`); the workspace audit log records the event, its method and time only. New invitation entries in the audit log record the invitation id and the address's domain, not the address. Entries written before keep it, because the audit chain can't be rewritten; they go with the audit log's age limit (below).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| The audit log                                   | Kept for **Settings > Privacy > Keep the audit log for** (1, 2 (default), 3 or 5 years, `auditLogMonths`), or less where the workspace's plan keeps it for a shorter time (the free plan keeps 30 days), on every plan including the top one. Every day older entries are removed in batches, an `audit.pruned` entry records where the log now starts, and what is left still verifies. The agent log follows the same limit (never under 90 days).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| A former teammate                               | An owner can remove the details of someone with no seat or publication role left in the workspace under **Settings > Privacy > Former teammates** (`POST /api/v1/workspaces/{workspaceId}/former-teammates/{operatorId}/redact`). Wherever the workspace shows who did something (the audit log and its export, posts and revisions, API keys, app grants, Copilot requests, data requests, approvals, and the agent log's "via Copilot" credential) it then shows a stable placeholder (`former-teammate-<12 hex>@redacted.invalid`) instead of their name and address, and their accepted invitations no longer hold the address. Audit entries are hash-chained, so they are not rewritten: a `teammate.redacted` entry records the change, the log is shown with the placeholder, and the stored entries go when they reach the audit log's age limit. Exported entries shown with the placeholder no longer match their hash outside Swarm; the in-product check still verifies. Their account and other workspaces are not changed. |

## For operators

- Per-person open and click tracking and reader profiles start **off** for every new publication (`NEW_CHANNEL_SETTINGS` in `packages/domain/src/tracking.ts`), so engagement scores, best-time sending, engagement segments and the inactive-reader cleanup do nothing there until you turn them on. Publications that existed before keep what they had: nothing is switched on or off for you by an update. Before you turn tracking on, get your readers' consent, and say in your privacy notice what you track, on what basis and for how long; readers can still turn it off for themselves on their subscriber settings page.
- Instance-wide, `engagement_scores` and `best_time_send` still switch those features off for every publication (**Settings > Instance > Features**). A publication switch can only turn things off further.
- API: `trackOpens`, `trackClicks` and `profileReaders` on `PATCH /api/v1/channels/{channelId}` (admin), and `GET`/`PUT /api/v1/workspaces/{workspaceId}/retention` (admin). Send stats carry `tracking: { opens, clicks, untracked }`.
- Data requests and erasure are covered in [GDPR](gdpr.md).
