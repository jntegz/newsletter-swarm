# Subprocessors

Swarm runs on these services outside its own code. A deployment sends email through one of Amazon SES, Resend, Mailgun or Postmark, or through the operator's own mail server. Each one processes personal data of Members (the people who read a Channel) only to provide Swarm, under its own data processing terms. Payments go through Stripe and, where a publication connects it, PayPal. Addresses are checked with DeBounce only when the person running the deployment sets it up. No other service receives Member data, and no third-party script runs on any Swarm page unless a publication's owner adds one to their website, where it waits for each reader's consent.

## What is not a subprocessor

- **Your own export destination.** When an operator connects their own R2 or S3 bucket, Swarm writes to it on the operator's instruction. That bucket is the operator's, not Swarm's.
- **Your own model key.** When a workspace saves its own Anthropic key for Copilot, its requests run under that Anthropic account, on the operator's instruction.
- **Your own mail server.** When a deployment sends over SMTP, that server is the operator's, not Swarm's.
- **Webhook receivers** an operator configures receive the events the operator subscribes them to, also on the operator's instruction.
- **Mailbox providers** (Gmail, Outlook and others) receive the email addressed to their users, as any email does.
